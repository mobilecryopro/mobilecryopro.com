Localized care

Treatment without a whole-body chamber

Localized cryotherapy applies controlled cold to a requested area instead of placing the whole body in a chamber. Dan explains the process and reviews basic health and safety information before treatment.

Appointments

Available for adults with different routines

Appointments are available for adults of different ages, activity levels, and routines, subject to health and safety screening. Tell Dan which area you want treated when you request a visit.

Questions

Common questions about localized cryotherapy

Is this a medical treatment?

Mobile Cryo Pro provides a wellness service, not medical care. For diagnosis, new injuries, or medical restrictions, consult a licensed healthcare professional first.

What areas can be treated?

Common requested areas include knees, shoulders, back, neck, elbows, hips, feet, and ankles.

Do you come to my home?

Yes. Mobile Cryo Pro serves homes, gyms, offices, hotels, and event spaces across Sonoma, Marin, and nearby North Bay communities.
