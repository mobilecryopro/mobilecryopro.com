The story

Why Dan built a mobile service

Dan created Mobile Cryo Pro to make localized cryotherapy and CryoSkin services available outside a clinic setting.

He brings the equipment, completes a brief intake, explains the service, and provides the appointment at the client’s location.

Clients work directly with Dan from the first conversation through the appointment.

Background

  • Certified in localized cryotherapy and CryoSkin 3.0
  • Training in sports recovery and aesthetic services
  • Mobile-equipment cleaning and safety procedures

What you get

  • One practitioner, start to finish
  • Service recommendations based on your stated goals
  • Pricing confirmed before the appointment
  • A private appointment at your location

Recovery

Localized treatment for specific muscles and joints

Appointments can focus on one or several areas. Dan reviews the area and your goals before treatment.

CryoSkin & Cryofacials

Body and facial services using CryoSkin 3.0
