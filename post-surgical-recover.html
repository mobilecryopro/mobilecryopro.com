Post-surgical recovery support

Post-surgical cryotherapy requires clinician approval.

Mobile Cryo Pro can provide localized cold therapy at your location after surgery only when your clinician has approved it and it is consistent with your recovery instructions.

Important Get approval from your clinician before requesting cryotherapy after surgery.

Private recovery

A private appointment at your location.

Mobile Cryo Pro can bring a compact setup to a home, hotel, or approved recovery space across Sonoma, Marin, and nearby North Bay communities.

Focused areas

Localized treatment with prior approval.

Localized cryotherapy applies controlled cold to a specific area rather than placing the whole body in a chamber. Dan follows the boundaries of your clinician’s approval and stated restrictions.

Medical guidance

Your clinician’s instructions come first.

Do not book until you have confirmed that cryotherapy is appropriate for your procedure, timing, treatment area, and current condition. Mobile Cryo Pro does not determine clinical suitability.

Scheduling

One visit or a planned series.

After clinical approval, Dan can confirm location, setup, availability, and pricing for one appointment or a planned series. Your clinician should determine treatment timing and frequency.

Questions

Before requesting a post-surgical visit.

Should I ask my doctor first?

Yes. After surgery, your clinician’s recovery instructions come first. Mobile Cryo Pro can work within appropriate guidance.

Can you come to my home?

Yes, if the location is workable and your clinician has approved treatment at that stage of recovery.

Is this only for athletes?

No. Eligibility depends on health and safety screening and, after surgery, your clinician’s approval.
