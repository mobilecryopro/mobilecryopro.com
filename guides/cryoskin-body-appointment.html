What to expect from a CryoSkin 3.0 body appointment.

The short version

What to know

  • CryoSkin 3.0 is a separate body-focused service, not whole-body cryotherapy.
  • Dan maps the requested area and confirms the plan before the appointment.
  • The sandbox does not promise inches lost, fat loss, or a particular visual result.

Choose the area you want to discuss

Mobile Cryo Pro lists a CryoSkin 3.0 body treatment for one mapped area, with an option to add another area during the same visit. Tell Dan the area you have in mind and what you want to know. He can help you choose the relevant service.

Confirm the service and price

The services page lists CryoSkin 3.0 at $225 for one mapped area and $120 for an additional area during the same visit. Confirm price and suitability before scheduling.

Contouring and de-puffing is listed separately. Similar names do not make the services interchangeable, so confirm which service is being quoted.

Plan for a private mobile setting

Body-focused appointments need an appropriate private space. Share whether the visit will be at a home, hotel, office, gym, or venue so Dan can confirm access and setup. The exact preparation, clothing, duration, and aftercare should come from Dan for the service being booked.

Keep expectations specific

Photos, testimonials, and descriptions can imply guaranteed or typical physical changes even when the wording is vague. This guide therefore does not promise inches lost, fat loss, permanent contour changes, or a particular result. Ask what the exact device is intended to do, what evidence supports that use, how progress would be measured, and what variability to expect.

What to include in your request

Send your city, treatment area, preferred timing, and whether you are considering one appointment or a series. Dan can then explain your options.

Talk with Dan before you book

Share the service, your location, and your questions. Dan will confirm options and availability.
