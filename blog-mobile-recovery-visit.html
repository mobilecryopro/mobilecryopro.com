Mobile visits

What a mobile recovery visit looks like.

Most visits require a private area, access to power, time for setup, and a short check-in with each person receiving a service.

May 2026 4 min read

The short version

Dan brings the equipment and supplies. You provide the agreed space, access to power, and any venue instructions.

Request a Visit

Mobile visits can take place at a gym, office, hotel, event, home, or other approved private location. Dan confirms the service, space, schedule, and number of people in advance.

Before the visit.

We confirm the address, parking, timing, and the number of people expected. If the visit is for a team, group, or event, Dan also creates a schedule so each participant has a time slot.

What we need from the space.

A power outlet and a quiet corner are enough for most visits. Privacy is required for CryoSkin and some treatment areas, so a separate room or screened area may be necessary.

Typical setup time:

About ten minutes once we are inside. We bring the equipment, treatment supplies, and cleaning materials. Venue access and parking can affect the final setup time.

How the session flows.

Each person completes a brief check-in before treatment. Dan confirms the requested service and area, reviews relevant health and safety information, and answers questions before proceeding.

Group and venue visits.

Gyms, training groups, offices, hotels, wellness events, and private groups can request a one-time visit or ask about a recurring schedule.
