# Sandbox photo review assets

These 13 active WebP files are derivatives of seven retained source photographs from Dan's manufacturer-provided library. They are intentionally scoped to `/sandbox` and are not referenced by the production site.

- Paired portrait comparisons use 4:5 framing. The neck pair uses the widest clean crop available from its tilted source panels, while the abdominal and upper-arm three-session series use aligned 3:2 framing so the complete treatment area remains visible.
- The neck source explicitly established its before/after order.
- The three-session files retain their source top-to-bottom order.
- The football-field photograph was excluded at Taylor's direction.
