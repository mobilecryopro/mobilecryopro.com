# Sandbox photo review assets

These 13 WebP files are the review-ready derivatives of seven retained source photographs from Dan's manufacturer-provided library. They are intentionally scoped to `/sandbox` and are not referenced by the production site.

- Comparison panels use a consistent 4:5 frame and contain no baked-in text or borders.
- The neck source explicitly established its before/after order.
- The one-session abdominal pair and both three-panel sets retain neutral review labels where source ordering still needs confirmation.
- The football-field photograph was excluded at Taylor's direction.
