The basics

Appointments and safety.

Do you offer whole-body chamber cryotherapy?

No. Mobile Cryo Pro focuses on localized cryotherapy and CryoSkin 3.0. More than one area can be treated in a visit, but no whole-body chamber is used.

What happens during a visit?

Dan arrives with the equipment, completes a brief intake, and reviews the requested service. Setup is usually about ten minutes, and the appointment length depends on the service and number of areas.

What safety screening do you do?

Dan completes a brief intake and reviews relevant health and safety information before treatment. He will not proceed if the service may be unsuitable and may ask you to consult a licensed medical professional.

Recovery

Localized cryotherapy.

What areas are commonly treated?

Common areas include shoulders, knees, hips, backs, elbows, feet, and other specific muscles or joints. More than one area can be treated during the same appointment.

When might I notice a difference?

Individual responses vary by person, treatment area, and reason for booking. Dan can explain what a single appointment involves and when clients commonly consider a series.

Does this replace PT or medical care?

No. Mobile Cryo Pro does not diagnose conditions or replace physical therapy, veterinary care, or treatment from a licensed medical professional.

CryoSkin & cryofacials

Body and facial services.

What CryoSkin 3.0 services do you offer?

Mobile Cryo Pro offers CryoSkin 3.0 body treatments and a CryoSkin facial. Dan reviews the requested area and explains the available service before booking.

What is the abdominal contouring option?

It is a lower-priced contouring and de-puffing service for one mapped area. It is separate from the CryoSkin 3.0 body treatment.

How many CryoSkin sessions do I need?

It depends on the selected service, treatment area, and your goals. Dan will review the area and discuss the proposed number and timing of appointments before you commit.

Can I book just a cryofacial?

Yes. Mobile Cryo Pro offers a CryoSkin 3.0 facial and a localized liquid-nitrogen cryofacial. Dan can explain the differences and confirm current pricing.

What should I expect from a cryofacial?

Dan explains the selected service before treatment. Individual appearance changes and timing vary, and no specific result is guaranteed.

Logistics & events

Travel, setup, and group visits.

What areas do you serve?

Sonoma and Marin are home base. Napa, Solano, and San Francisco are available by request. You can also see the service-area map on the homepage.

What do you need to set up?

Most appointments need a power outlet and enough clear space for the equipment. Dan confirms access, parking, privacy, and setup details before the visit.

Can you do weddings, retreats, or office events?

Yes. Contact Dan with the location, estimated number of guests, preferred services, and event date for a custom quote.

Do you work with horses?

Yes, by request. See the equine cryotherapy FAQs for barn-side setup, use cases, and booking details.
