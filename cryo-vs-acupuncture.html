Recovery comparison

Localized cryotherapy and acupuncture are different services.

Localized cryotherapy applies controlled cold to a requested area. Acupuncture is a separate needle-based practice provided by a qualified practitioner.

Ask What Fits Localized Cryotherapy
Quick answer Mobile Cryo Pro provides cryotherapy. It does not provide acupuncture or advise which clinical treatment you need.

Cryotherapy

Controlled cold applied to a specific area.

Mobile Cryo Pro provides localized cryotherapy for requested areas such as knees, shoulders, back, neck, and feet. Dan brings the equipment to your approved private location.

Acupuncture

A different kind of appointment.

Acupuncture uses thin needles placed at selected points and should be performed by a properly qualified practitioner. Ask that practitioner or your healthcare provider whether it is appropriate for you.

Mobile service

Cryotherapy at your location.

Mobile Cryo Pro comes to your home, gym, office, hotel, event space, or other approved private location. Acupuncture is not part of the appointment.

Medical decisions

Ask a licensed professional when needed.

Mobile Cryo Pro does not diagnose conditions or recommend one clinical treatment over another. If you have an injury, condition, or medical restriction, ask a licensed healthcare professional what is appropriate.
